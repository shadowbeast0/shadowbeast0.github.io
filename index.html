No. 002PsychicGhost

SAGED-Net

The Nucleus Hunter build

Published · IEEE IJCNN 2026

A lightweight network that outlines every cell nucleus in histopathology slides. Learnable adaptive gating filters out background noise and a tri-domain fidelity loss keeps boundaries sharp, beating state-of-the-art results on TNBC, CPM and PanNuke. Published at IEEE IJCNN / WCCI 2026 in the Netherlands.

  • PyTorch
  • Segmentation
  • Histopathology
